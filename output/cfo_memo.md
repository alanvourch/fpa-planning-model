# CFO memo: headcount, cloud and product investment, next 18 months

To: CEO, VP Engineering, Board finance committee. From: FP&A. Date: 2026-09-17. Model: assumption set v1.3, generated from the planning model, no figure typed by hand.

## Decision

Adopt the phased hiring plan with a one-year cloud commitment: 22 hires between October 2026 and May 2027 (14 in R&D, 7 in sales and marketing, 1 in G&A), and a one-year cloud commitment of 444,820 compute units a month at a 25% discount. Hold the front-loaded plan and the Insights contractor push until net retention recovers.

## Where we stand

ARR is USD 24.3M at end August 2026, up 41% year on year, with USD 24.8M of cash and a trailing net burn of USD 762k a month (32 months of runway). Gross margin is 72.2% against 74.2% budgeted for the month: cloud hosting has risen from 7.9% of revenue in February to 9.3% because Insights customers consume 32% more compute. Year to date, EBITDA is +USD 459k against budget: late engineering hires (+USD 411k) and fewer new customers (-USD 561k of platform revenue) offset each other, while cloud usage (-USD 104k) is the one variance that changes the plan.

## Why this option

- 6 options were run through the same drivers under base, upside and downside (18 runs). Two guardrails apply: downside runway never below 18 months, and base gross margin never below 74%.
- The front-loaded plan (31 hires plus contractors) adds only USD 1.7M of ARR by February 2028 in the base case and breaches the runway guardrail in the downside: 16.9 months, 1.1 short of the 18-month floor, with cash bottoming at USD 10.7M.
- Holding hiring keeps USD 4.5M more cash but ends USD 2.2M lower in ARR, and leaves the Insights product without the squad that its usage growth is paying for.
- Cloud on demand fails the margin floor (base minimum 72.9%). The commitment saves USD 0.8M over 18 months in the base case and lifts the minimum gross margin by 2.0 points; the downside strands USD 178k of committed units, which is the price of the floor.

## Risks

- Net retention. The base assumes 105%; the downside 96%. Each review of the change log has moved this driver down, not up.
- Sales productivity. New customers per ramped account executive are 0.90 a month in the base and 0.50 in the downside; the 7 go-to-market hires only pay back if the base holds.
- R&D weight. R&D is 52% of revenue today, well above the benchmark range, which is why hiring is phased rather than front-loaded.

## Asks

1. Approve the phased hiring plan and the 14 R&D requisitions in the order listed.
2. Approve the one-year cloud commitment before the next renewal window.
3. Revisit the front-loaded plan at the Q1 2027 review if net retention is at or above the base assumption.
